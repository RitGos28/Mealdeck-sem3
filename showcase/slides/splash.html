MealDeck
Menus for every food stall on campus.
  • Every stall's menu and prices in one place: 8 stalls, 1,086 dishes.
  • Search that handles typos. "panner tika" still finds Paneer Tikka.
  • See what's sold out before you walk over.
  • Mess menu from a MyCamu screenshot, read on your phone.
  • Can't decide? Deal me a dish, which learns what you like.
This is a demo, built by students. Bennett is mentioned only because the menus are from its campus stalls; MealDeck is not endorsed or sponsored by Bennett University.
