MealDeckHow it works

The ML, and how we tested it

No LLMs. Each model is small enough to run on a laptop or in the browser.

01

Search that handles typos

Search panner tika or manchoorian and you still get the right dish. It's a character-trigram model built from all 1,086 dishes. We tested it on 39 misspelled searches.

Plain search found it26%
MealDeck, ranked first87%
MealDeck, in the top 10100%
02

Deal me a dish

You swipe through 10 dishes and keep or skip each one. It uses online logistic regression, with one model for everyone plus a personal one, updated after each hand.

You can turn it off in Account, which also deletes your swipes.
03

Mess menu from a screenshot

Pick your MyCamu mess-menu screenshots and the day's four meals get filled in. Tesseract OCR reads them in the browser, then our parser splits the text into meals.

The images aren't uploaded anywhere.
04

Section suggestions for stalls

When a vendor adds a dish, we suggest which section it goes in, using k-nearest neighbours on their existing menu.

First guess right 69% of the time (14% if you always pick the biggest section)
05

Similar, for less

Shows cheaper similar dishes at other stalls, using the same similarity as search. Veg only matches veg, and half plates aren't matched with full ones.

29% of dishes have one, e.g. Oreo shake ₹147 → ₹85
06

Pickup-time prediction simulated data

A random forest estimates how long an order will take. The biggest factor is how many orders are ahead of yours.

Average error 2.1 min (6.1 min if you just guess the average)
07

Mess guesses

If nobody has added today's menu yet, we guess from the same day in past weeks and mark it "Probably".
